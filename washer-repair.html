Diagnostic Checklist

Common Washer Problems We Repair

We solve the mechanical and electronic faults that stop washing machines from completing their cycles.

!

Washer Not Spinning

Defective door/lid safety lock mechanism, snapped drive belt, worn motor drive coupling, or faulty rotor position sensor.

!

Excessive Shaking & Banging

Failed suspension dampening rods, broken tub counterweight springs, loose floor pedestal mounts, or degraded rear tub bearings.

!

Water Not Draining (OE / 5E Error)

Coins, socks, or debris jammed in the drain filter housing, cracked drain impeller, or burned-out drain motor windings.

!

Water Leaking Around the Front or Base

Punctured front door boot bellow, loose internal water pressure switch hose, cracked tub seam, or deteriorated water inlet valve.

!

Machine Stops Mid-Cycle

Electronic control board relay fault, unbalance detection trip, water level pressure sensor error, or thermal overload shutdown.

!

Washer Won't Fill With Water

Clogged water inlet screen filters, defective dual solenoid valves, or a malfunctioning water temperature mixing sensor.

Diagnostic Routine

What Our Washer Repair Includes

When our technician visits your home in San Jose, we perform a thorough assessment:

  • Error code retrieval and live diagnostic mode cycle analysis
  • Drive belt tension check, motor rotor inspection, and stator winding tests
  • Drain pump flow rate verification and foreign object trap clearing
  • Water inlet valve testing and internal water level pressure sensor calibration
  • Post-repair operational spin, agitation, and water drain test

What to Do Before Calling

Check both hot and cold wall water valves are fully open, verify the drain hose is not kinked behind the unit, and note down the model number.

Avoid Further Damage

If the washer is violently shaking or leaking onto laundry room flooring, pause the cycle and disconnect power until an inspection can be conducted.
