Symptoms & Fixes

Common Dishwasher Problems We Repair

We solve the mechanical, hydraulic, and electrical malfunctions that stop dishwashers from working.

!

Water Not Draining

Clogged sump filters, jammed drain pump impellers, malfunctioning check valves, or blocked drain hose loops.

!

Water Leaking Onto the Kitchen Floor

Degraded perimeter door gaskets, loose hose clamps, defective float switches allowing overfill, or cracked wash arms.

!

Dishes Coming Out Dirty

Clogged spray arm water jets, failing circulation wash motor, or low water temperature caused by a burned heating element.

!

Dishwasher Won't Start or Turn On

Defective door latch switches failing to register closure, blown thermal fuses, or a malfunctioning electronic control board.

!

Loud Grinding or Humming Noise

Hard food objects or broken glass caught in the chopper blade assembly, or worn wash pump bearings.

!

Detergent Dispenser Not Opening

Broken wax motor actuator, failed solenoid spring mechanism, or detergent door hinge obstruction.

Comprehensive Evaluation

What Our Dishwasher Repair Covers

Our technicians follow a methodical diagnostic checklist on every dishwasher service call:

  • Inspection of drain pump motor, check valve, and plumbing disposal tie-in
  • Circulation motor amperage check and wash pressure testing
  • Heating element continuity and high-limit thermostat evaluation
  • Door latch sensor alignment and seal watertight testing
  • Post-repair test fill, wash cycle, and drain operation verification

What to Do Before Calling

Ensure the garbage disposal is clear of food debris, verify the under-sink water supply valve is turned on, and check for tripped circuit breakers.

Prevent Water Damage

If you notice active water leaking beneath the dishwasher, shut off the dedicated water valve under the kitchen sink to prevent cabinet floor water damage.
