Thermal Precision

Restoring Even Heating & Accurate Baking Temperatures

Baking and roasting rely on precise thermodynamic cycles. Modern ovens utilize electronic control relays to alternate between the lower bake element and the upper broil element, guided by an RTD resistance temperature detector probe.

Whether you operate a built-in single wall oven, double oven configuration, or an integrated cooking chamber, our San Jose technicians carry precision instruments to test heating elements, calibrate thermistors, and check safety gas valves.

Failure Symptoms

Common Oven Problems We Repair

If your oven is exhibiting any of these symptoms, our technicians can evaluate and resolve the issue safely.

!

Oven Not Heating at All

Burned-out bake heating element with visible blistering, weak hot-surface gas glow igniter, or a tripped high-limit thermal safety fuse.

!

Baking Food Unevenly

Drifting temperature sensor probe resistance, convection fan motor failure, or a burned broil element preventing temperature cycling.

!

Gas Oven Takes Very Long to Light

A worn gas igniter that glows orange but draws insufficient electrical amperage (under 3.2 amps) to open the bi-metal gas safety valve.

!

Oven Door Won't Close Tightly or Unlock

Worn or bent heavy door hinges, damaged door seal perimeter gaskets letting heat escape, or a jammed self-cleaning latch motor.

!

Oven Overheating or Burning Meals

Stuck closed control board relay continuously feeding 240V power to the heating element regardless of the selected temperature setpoint.

!

Error Codes on Digital Display (F1, F3, F9)

Control panel communication errors, shorted RTD temperature sensor circuit, or cooling fan airflow interlock switch trip.

Service Breakdown

What Our Oven Repair Service Includes

When our technician visits your San Jose home, we conduct an exhaustive electrical and mechanical diagnostic:

  • Ohmmeter resistance measurement across bake, broil, and convection elements
  • Current amperage draw testing on gas glow bar igniters and safety valves
  • RTD temperature sensor calibration check at ambient and elevated temperatures
  • Inspection of electronic clock relay contacts and cooling fan motors
  • Post-repair operational temperature verification test cycle

What to Do Before Calling

Inspect the bake element for physical blistering or breaks, confirm the oven clock is set (some digital models require this), and check breakers.

Avoid Self-Clean Cycle

If an oven is already behaving erratically, running the ultra-high temperature self-clean cycle can permanently destroy sensitive electronic boards.
