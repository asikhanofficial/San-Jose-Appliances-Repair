Diagnostic Overview

Common Range Problems We Repair

We resolve the intricate electrical and mechanical problems that prevent ranges from performing properly.

!

Cooktop Works But Oven Doesn't

Burnt bake or broil element, failed oven gas igniter, or stuck relay on the central electronic control board.

!

Oven Works But Surface Burners Don't

Burned terminal block connection, failed spark ignition harness, or bad rotary surface control switches.

!

Range Tripping Household Breaker

Shorted heating element wire contacting the steel frame, or loose high-amperage 240V terminal block lugs causing arc faults.

!

Inaccurate Temperature Calibration

Degraded oven thermistor resistance value causing the computer board to shut heating elements off prematurely.

!

Range Door Hinges Loose or Squeaking

Worn counterbalancing springs or bent hinge receivers preventing the heavy glass oven door from creating an airtight thermal seal.

!

Digital Control Clock Blank or Beeping

Power surge damage to the lower low-voltage power supply board or failed capacitive touch membrane keyboard.

Service Protocol

What Our Range Repair Service Covers

When our technician inspects your kitchen range in San Jose, we conduct an exhaustive dual-system diagnostic:

  • Main 240V power cord connection and terminal block thermal inspection
  • Resistance testing on all surface burners and interior bake/broil elements
  • Oven cavity temperature probe calibration and electronic relay testing
  • Gas regulator pressure checks and spark safety ignition verification
  • Post-repair operational safety testing of cooktop burners and oven bake cycle

What to Do Before Calling

Note your range's model and serial number (usually on a metal plate visible behind the lower warming drawer or along the oven door frame).

Check Circuit Breaker

Electric ranges use a double-pole 40A or 50A breaker. Sometimes one side trips, allowing the clock to stay on while heating elements receive no power.
