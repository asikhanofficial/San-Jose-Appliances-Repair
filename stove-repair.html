Reliable Cooktop & Surface Burner Solutions

Surface cooking units experience heavy daily exposure to heat expansion, boiling liquids, grease, and high-frequency knob adjustments. Gas units require precise air-to-fuel mixtures and intact ceramic spark electrodes, while electric smooth-top surfaces depend on infinite heat rotary switches and high-temperature thermal limiters.

Symptom Checker

Common Stove Problems We Repair

If your stove or cooktop is malfunctioning, our technicians can evaluate and restore the unit.

!

Gas Burner Won't Light (No Spark)

Failed spark ignition module, cracked ceramic electrode insulator, broken harness wiring, or clogged burner head gas ports.

!

Igniter Won't Stop Clicking

Moisture or cleaning residue shorting the rotary burner knob microswitch, or a defective electronic spark box continuously pulsing.

!

Electric Surface Element Not Heating

Burned infinite heat rotary switch, melted internal wire terminal connector, or defective radiant glass ribbon element.

!

Burner Flame is Weak or Yellow

Restricted brass burner jet orifice, improper air shutter adjustment, or low gas pressure regulator flow.

!

Burner Stays on High Heat Only

Stuck infinite control bimetal contacts unable to cycle power off, feeding continuous 240V voltage to the element.

!

Hot Surface Indicator Light Stays On

Defective residual heat thermostat sensor under the ceramic glass top failing to open contacts when cooled.

What to Do Before Calling

Ensure burner caps are properly aligned on the burner heads, verify the gas shutoff valve behind the cooktop is open, and check that burner heads are completely dry.

Smell Gas? Act Immediately

If you detect a distinct natural gas odor, do not turn on any light switches. Turn off the main gas valve, open windows, and call your utility company immediately.
