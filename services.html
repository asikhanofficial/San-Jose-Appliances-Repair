How We Evaluate Appliance Failures

Modern household appliances have evolved into sophisticated electromechanical devices. Randomly swapping parts without testing leads to unnecessary expenses and unresolved issues.

  • Error code retrieval and live sensor data verification
  • Ohmmeter resistance checks on heating elements and motor coils
  • Water pressure, flow rate, and drain line restriction analysis
  • Sealed refrigeration temperature delta evaluation
